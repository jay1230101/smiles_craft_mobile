## 1. Who this policy is for

Smiles Craft is a professional clinic-management tool used by **authorized dental clinic personnel**
— for example clinic owners, dentists, assistants, cashiers and administrators. It is not a consumer
or patient-facing app and is not intended for use by the general public.

This policy addresses two groups of people:

- **App users** — the clinic staff who sign in to and operate the App.
- **Patients** — individuals whose personal and health information is entered and managed within the
  App by clinic staff as part of the clinic’s care and record-keeping.

Each clinic that uses the Service is responsible for the patient information it enters and for
obtaining any consents required under applicable law before recording that information.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the Service. This includes:

**a. Account and identity information**
- Email address and password used to sign in (the password is transmitted securely to our servers
  for verification and is **not** stored on your device).
- Your name, professional role, clinic association and account settings.
- An authentication token issued after sign-in, stored in the device’s protected/secure storage so
  you stay signed in.

**b. Patient information (entered by clinic staff)**
- Patient name, date of birth, gender, phone number and known allergies.
- The clinician assigned to the patient.

**c. Clinical and health information**
- Appointments and scheduling details.
- Clinical encounters, diagnoses, treatment plans, plan-of-care notes and related documentation
  recorded during the course of care.

**d. Billing and financial information**
- Invoices, procedure charges, payments recorded and outstanding balances.
- The App does not process card numbers or bank account details.

**e. Communications information**
- Appointment reminders and confirmations sent to patients, including the destination phone number
  and the message content, where the clinic chooses to send them.

**f. Device and technical information**
- Device model and operating-system version, App version and language.
- A push-notification token used to deliver notifications to your device.
- Log and diagnostic data and the IP address used to connect to our servers, collected to keep the
  Service secure and reliable.

> We do **not** use the Service to display third-party advertising, and we do **not** sell or rent
> personal information to anyone.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- Provide and operate the App’s scheduling, patient-management, clinical-documentation and billing
  features.
- Authenticate users, keep accounts secure and enforce role-based access within each clinic.
- Send appointment reminders and confirmations and deliver operational notifications.
- Keep information synchronized in real time across a clinic’s authorized devices and staff.
- Maintain the clinical and financial records the clinic needs for care and administration.
- Diagnose problems, prevent fraud or misuse, and improve the reliability and security of the
  Service.
- Comply with our legal, regulatory and professional record-keeping obligations.

We do not use patient health information for advertising or for any purpose unrelated to providing
the Service to the clinic.

## 4. Our legal bases

Where data-protection law (such as the GDPR or equivalent local law) applies, we rely on the
following legal bases: performance of our contract with the clinic; our legitimate interests in
operating and securing the Service; compliance with legal obligations; and, where required for
health information, the clinic’s role as a healthcare provider and any consent it obtains from
patients. The clinic acts as the controller of the patient information it enters, and we act as a
processor handling that information on the clinic’s behalf.

## 5. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Hosting and database infrastructure provider | Storing and processing Service data on secure servers. |
| Push-notification delivery services (the platform’s push service and Apple / Google notification gateways) | Delivering notifications to your device. |
| Messaging provider used for appointment reminders | Sending reminder and confirmation messages the clinic chooses to send. |
| Authorized staff within the same clinic | Collaborating on scheduling, care and billing for that clinic’s patients. |

These providers may process information only on our instructions and are bound to protect it. We may
also disclose information if required to do so by law, to comply with a legal process, to protect the
rights, property or safety of patients, staff or the public, or in connection with a merger,
acquisition or reorganization (subject to this policy). Information is kept separated by clinic — one
clinic cannot access another clinic’s data.

## 6. How we protect information

- Data is transmitted between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Sign-in tokens are held in the device’s protected/secure storage rather than in plain files.
- Access is restricted to authenticated users and governed by role-based permissions within each
  clinic.
- We limit access to personal and health information to those who need it to operate or support the
  Service.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect information using
measures appropriate to its sensitivity.

## 7. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as an account or clinic relationship is active and for as long as
needed to provide the Service. Clinical and financial records may be retained for longer where the
clinic is required to keep them under applicable healthcare, tax or professional record-keeping laws.
When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymize it. Specific retention periods are set
by the clinic in line with its legal obligations in [GOVERNING_JURISDICTION].
